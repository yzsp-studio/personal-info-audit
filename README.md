## 🇬🇧 English

A compliance-audit workflow for China's personal information protection rules: it judges the audit trigger, registers evidence and rates its probative value, then works through facts, uncertainty, data classification, processing-activity inventory, legal roles, processing scenarios and applicable rules across 26 modules and 107 sub-items, and designs risks and remediation. Every conclusion traces backward through an 11-stage chain — evidence, facts, uncertainty, data classification, processing activities, legal role, processing scenario, applicable rules, audit conclusion, risk, remediation — and a single broken link downgrades it to 'undeterminable' with the minimum additional evidence listed. Outputs are a traceable Word audit report and Excel working papers covering PIPL, network data security, apps and SDKs, sensitive personal information, minors, facial recognition, automated decision-making, AI, entrusted sharing, cross-border transfer and regulatory inspection readiness. Findings are graded L0 to L4, and a formal audit opinion is only issued once evidence is sufficient, the chain is complete and quality review passes. All deliverables are review drafts for lawyers or compliance professionals, not a substitute for legal judgement.

### ✨ What it does

- Trigger and scenario judgement: decide whether an audit is triggered, at what frequency and to whom it is reported, and grade the deliverable L0 to L4
- Evidence register and probative-value rating: log materials, rate their weight, and flag conflicts and broken links
- 11-stage fact-analysis chain: leave a trace at every stage from evidence to remediation, downgrading to 'undeterminable' when a link breaks
- 26-module, 107-sub-item working papers: score every sub-item into a complete Excel workbook
- High-risk specials: AI, facial recognition, automated decision-making, minors, cross-border transfer, entrusted sharing
- Deliverables: a traceable Word audit report plus Excel working papers, gated by quality review and sign-off
